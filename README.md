> **Your den is waiting. Game tonight.**

Finding compatible people to play co-op with is harder than it should be — especially when your friends are offline. Dengate is a social matchmaking platform built for co-op gamers: it pairs you with compatible players, puts you in a voice room together, and gets you into a game.

No Discord. No third-party tools. Just matchmaking, voice chat, and the people you want to play with.

**[dengate.xyz](https://dengate.xyz)**

---

## What you get

- **Matchmaking** — Get paired with compatible players by game and language preference
- **Voice rooms** — Built-in voice chat, native to the platform
- **Direct messaging** — Real-time chat with friends
- **Game library** — See what your matches own and play via Steam sync
- **Explorer** — Browse and discover other gamers' public profiles
- **Blog** — Co-op game recommendations and reviews to help you decide what to play next
